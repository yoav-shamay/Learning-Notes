$$
\begin{matrix}
1 && 2 \\
3 && 4 \\
\end{matrix}
$$
`&&` is used for a space and `\\` for newline
You can use `\dots` to insert ellipsis (...) in the middle
Matrix parenthesis:
- `matrix` - no parenthesis
- `pmatrix` - with parenthesis
- `bmatrix` - with brackets
- `vmatrix` with bar (|) (determinant)
