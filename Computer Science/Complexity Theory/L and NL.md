$L$ = every language decidable in log space
$NL$ = every language decidable in nondeterministic log space
## NL-Complete Language
Languages $L$ satisfying $L \in NL$ and for every $L' \in NL$, $L'$ is reducible in log-space ([[Reductions#Many-one reduction|Many-one Reduction]]) to $L$
### List
- $PATH$ - whether there's a path between 2 vertices in graph.
