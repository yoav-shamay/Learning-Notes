## No large primes
Pohlig Hellman solves discrete logarithm in $\sum_{p|n} \sqrt{p}$ for a group of order $n$. If all factors of the group order are small, then the curve is weak.
## Curves with trace of Frobenius one
Curves over $\mathbb{Z}_p$ of order $p$.
