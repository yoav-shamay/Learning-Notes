## Existence (Euler Criteria)
$x$ is a quadratic residue (there exists a square root) mod $p$ iff $x^{(p-1)/2} \equiv 1 \pmod p$
## $p \equiv 3 \pmod 4$
In this case, $(p - 1)/2$ is odd, so there exists inverse for 2 mod $(p - 1) / 2$ and it's exactly $(p + 1) / 4$.
The square root is $\pm x^{(p+1)/4}$.
As $(x^{(p+1)/4})^2 = x^{(p-1)/2+1} = x$
