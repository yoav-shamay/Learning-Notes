A protocol for sharing large files.
The idea of this protocol that it allows downloading from multiple sources in parallel, allowing for faster downloads.
While downloading you also usually share the file you downloaded with others, creating multiple sources to download from for others. You share the parts you download after downloading, and usually after finishing to download you still share until some limit (TODO elaborate).
## Terminology
- Seed - someone who has finished downloading (has the entire file) and is uploading for others.
- Peer - any part of the swarm, who connects to other computers, usually refers to someone who haven't downloaded 100% of the file and wants to download it.
- Leecher - usually refers to a downloader, someone who doesn't have 100% of the file
