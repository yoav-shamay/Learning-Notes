For $p_n$ that converges to $p$, with $p_n \neq p$ for all $n$, if
$\lim_{n \to \infty} |p_{n+1} - p| / |p_n - p|^\alpha = \lambda$
Then we say $p_n$ converges to $p$ of order $\alpha$ with asymptotic error constant $\lambda$.
If $\alpha = 1$ (and $\lambda < 1$) we say the sequence is linearly convergent.
If $\alpha = 2$ we say the sequence is quadratically convergent.
