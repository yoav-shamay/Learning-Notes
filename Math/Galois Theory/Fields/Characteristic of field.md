The characteristic of $F$ is the minimum positive $x \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $x \times 1 = 0$ (repeated addition of 1) or zero if no such
It's denoted $\operatorname{char} F$.
Also defined for a general ring (but the properties listed here aren't true for a general ring)
- Always a prime or zero
- If it's a prime $p$, $\mathbb{Z}_p$ is a minimal subfield of $F$ - a subfield of every subfield of $F$
- If it's zero, $\mathbb{Q}$ is a minimal subfield of $F$
- If it's a prime, $f(x) = x^p$ is a homomorphism from $F$ to $F$ (it's identity on $F_p$)
- For a field with characteristic zero, irreducible polynomials can't have multiple root
- For a field with characteristic $p \neq 0$, $(a + b)^{p^n} = a^{p^n} + b^{p^n}$
