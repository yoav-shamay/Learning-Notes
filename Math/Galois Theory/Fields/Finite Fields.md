For every prime $p$ and $\mathbb{N} \ni n > 0$, there exists a unique field (up to isomorphism) of order $p^n$.
- This field is the [[Splitting Field]] of $x^{p^n} - x$, and in fact is exactly all roots of the polynomial.
- This field has [[Characteristic of field|characteristic]] $p$.
- The [[Multiplicative Group of a Field|multiplicative group]] of the field is cyclic.
- Every extension $[K : F]$ where $K$ is finite is a simple extension (of the form $K(a)$), as the generator can be added.
