Given a field $F$ and $p(x) \in F[x]$, an extension field $M$ of $F$ is called a splitting field of $f(x)$ over $F$ if $f(x)$ factors into linear factors over $M$ and doesn't factor to linear factors over any non-degenerate subfield of $M$.
## Properties
- Every polynomial has a splitting field
- All splitting fields of a polynomial are isomorphic
- $[M : F]\leq n!$ where $n = \deg p(x)$
- Given an isomorphism $f : F \to \overline{F}$, and $\overline{M}$ a splitting field of $f(p(x))$, then $f$ can be extended to an isomorphism between $M$ and $\overline{M}$.
- An extension is a finite normal extension iff it's a splitting field
