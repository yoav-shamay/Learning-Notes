An extension $K / F$ is normal iff every irreducible polynomial over $F$ that has a root in $K$, factors to linear factors over $K$.
## Properties
- $K / F$ is a finite normal extension iff $K$ is the [[Splitting Field]] of a polynomial on $F$.
- Every extension of order 2 is normal
- If $K / F, K / L, L / F$ are field extensions and $K / F$ is normal than $K / L$ is normal
