## Equalizer
Given 2 rings $R, S$ and 2 homomorphisms $f, g : G \rightarrow S$
The equalizer of $f$ and $g$ is $\{x \in R | f(x) = g(x)\}$
Always a subring of $R$.

If $R$ is a field, $S$ is an integral domain and $f$ and $g$ aren't zero, the equalizer is a subfield.
## Fixed Field
Given a field $K$ and an endomorphism $f: K \rightarrow K$, the equalizer of $f$ and the identity homomorphism is a subfield of $K$ and called the fixed field of $f$.
This can also be defined for a set of homomorphisms, defined as the intersection of the fixed field of each homomorphisms.
For a set $A$, it's denoted $K^A$.

This can also be defined for a field extension $K / F$ with homomorphisms $f: F \rightarrow K$.
