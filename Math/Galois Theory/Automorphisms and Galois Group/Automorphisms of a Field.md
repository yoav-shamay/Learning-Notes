An invertible homomorphism from a field to itself.
## Automorphism Group of a field
All automorphisms of a field form a group with composition,
- Given a finite set of automorphisms $A$ on a field $K$, denote $F = K^A$, then $[K:F] \geq |A|$
- If $A$ form a group (a subgroup of the automorphisms group) then $[K:F] = |A|$
## Linear Combination of Automorphisms
For $a_1, ..., a_n \in F$ and $f_1, ..., f_n$ automorphisms, you can define the linear combination $a_1f_1 + ... + a_nf_n$.
This is a function from the field to itself, not necessarily a homomorphism.
- If $a_1f_1 + ... + a_nf_n = 0$ (the zero function) then $a_1 = ... = a_n = 0$.
