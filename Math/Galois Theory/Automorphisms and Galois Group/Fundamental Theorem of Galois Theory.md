Given a [[Galois Extensions]] $K / F$, denote $\varphi, \psi$ the [[Galois Group#Galois Correspondence|Galois Corresepondence Maps]], and $F \subseteq L \subseteq K$ a middle field. then:
1. $|G(K, F)| = [K : F]$
2.  $\varphi, \psi$ are the inverse of each other, and if $A \subseteq B$ then $\varphi(B) \subseteq \varphi(A)$
3. $[L : F]  =[G(K, F) : G(K, L)] = [\varphi(F) : \varphi(L)]$, $|\varphi(L)| = [K : L]$
4.  $L$ is a normal extension of $F$ iff $\varphi(L) = G(K, L)$ is a normal subgroup of $G(K, F)$
5. If $L$ is a normal extension of $F$, then $G(L, F) \cong G(K, L) / \varphi(L)$
