Given a group $G$, the commutator subgroup $G'$ of $G$ is defined as $\{aba^{-1}b^{-1} | a, b \in G\}$
## Properties
- if $G$ is abelian, $G' = \{e\}$
- $G^{(i)}$ is normal in $G$ (apply commutator $i$ times )
- $G / G'$ is abelian
- If $N$ is a normal subgroup of $G$, then $G / N$ is abelian iff $G' \subseteq N$
- $G$ is solvable iff there exists an $i \in \mathbb{N}$ such that $G^{(i)} = \{e\}$
