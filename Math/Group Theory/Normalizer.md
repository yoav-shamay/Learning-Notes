For a subgroup $H \subseteq G$, and a nonempty subset $A \subseteq G$:
The normalizer of A in H is defined as $N_H(A) = \{h \in H | h^{-1}Ah = A\}$.
## Properties
- $N_H(A)$ is a subgroup of $G$.
- $N_H(A) = H \cap N_G(A)$
- $[H : N_H(A)] = |H| / |N_H(A)| = n_A$, where $n_A = |\{h^-1Ah | h \in H\}|$
- For a Sylow p-subgroup $A$ (and $H=G$), $n_A$ is the amount of Sylow p-subgroups.
