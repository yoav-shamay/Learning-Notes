$S_n$ is the group of all permutations of length $n$, with concatenation as multiplication.
It's called the symmetric group.
$A_n$ is the subgroup of $S_n$ of all even permutations.
It's called the alternating group.
## Normal Subgroups
- For $n \geq 5$ the only normal subgroups of $S_n$ are $\{e\}, A_n, S_n$
- $A_n$ is simple for $n \geq 5$
