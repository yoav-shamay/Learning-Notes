Given $a \in R^k$ and $r \in R$:
- The open ball of radius $r$ around $a$ is $B(a;r) = \{x \in R^k | |x - a| < r\}$
- The closed ball of radius $r$ around $a$ is $B(a;r) = \{x \in R^k | |x - a| \leq r\}$
- The sphere of radius $r$ around $a$ is $S(a;r) = \{x \in R^k | |x - a| = r\}$
## Properties
- Every open / closed ball is convex
- The open line segment between any 2 points in a sphere is disjoint to the sphere, and fully within the matching open ball
- An open ball union any subset of the matching sphere is convex
