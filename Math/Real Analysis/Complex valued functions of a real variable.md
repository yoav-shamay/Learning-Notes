Functions $f : \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$.
It's of the form $f(x) = u(x) + iv(x)$.
$u(x)$ is called the real part of $f(x)$ and denoted $\operatorname{Re}{f(x)}$
$v(x)$ is called the imaginary part of $f(x)$ and denoted $\operatorname{Im}{f(x)}$
If $u(x)$ and $v(x)$ are differentiable then $f(x)$ is and $f'(x) = u'(x) + iv'(x)$.
## Exponential functions
For a complex $\lambda = \alpha + i\beta$, $e^{\lambda x} = e^{\alpha x}(\cos{\beta x} + i\sin{\beta x})$
