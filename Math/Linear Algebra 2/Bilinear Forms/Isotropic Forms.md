## Definition
An isotropic quadratic form is a quadratic form $q : V \rightarrow F$ such that exists $v \neq 0$ satisfying $q(v) = 0$
## Properties
- if $q = a_1x_1^2 + ... + a_nx_n^2$ under basis $w$, then $q$ is isotropic iff there exists a non trivial solution to $a_1x_1^2 + ... + a_nx_n^2 = 0$
- On $V$ on $\mathbb{C}$ satisfying $\dim{V} > 1$ every form is isotropic
- on $V$ on $\mathbb{R}$ a non-zero form is isotropic iff it's negative/positive semidefinite or non-sign preserving.
