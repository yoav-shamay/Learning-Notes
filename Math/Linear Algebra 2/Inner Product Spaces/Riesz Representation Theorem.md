## Statement
Given an inner product space $V$ on field $F$, and a transformation $T: V \rightarrow F$:
There's a vector $k \in V$ satisfying $T(v) = (v, k)$.
If $F = \mathbb{R}$ then the transformation $k \rightarrow (v, k)$ is an isomorphism from $V$ to $\hat{T}$, and if $F = \mathbb{C}$ it's an anti-isomorphism.
