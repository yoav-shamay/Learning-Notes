The inner product space of all continuous functions on $[a, b]$.
The norm is $\int_a^b f(x)g(x) dx$, or alternatively $\int f(x)g(x)\rho(x) dx$ with some cost function $\rho$.
## Results
- Every orthonormal series of polynomials (under any cost function $\rho$) where the $n$th polynomial is of degree $n$, is a complete system.
