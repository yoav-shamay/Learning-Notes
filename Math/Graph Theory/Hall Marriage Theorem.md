There exists a perfect matching from $A$ to $B$ in a bipartite graph iff for each subset $S \subseteq A$ there are at least $|S|$ elements in $B$ connected to something in $S$ 
## Generalization - vertex with many demands
If the vertex $i$ in $A$ has demand $k_i$, this is still true - iff there are at least $\sum S$ elements in $B$ connected to something in $S$.
